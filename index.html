About us

A specialist carrier for demanding cargo

Founded in 2017, Anand Transport is a Mithapur, Gujarat-based logistics company built around cargo that needs specialist handling: steel coils, long pipes, scrap, bulk commodities, containers and over-dimensional loads.

Our own fleet of 20 flatbed and coil trailers, backed by 100+ attached vehicles, lets us scale quickly. Customers deal with one accountable partner from the loading point to the final site.

Coils, plates, TMT and structurals on coil cradles with chain lashing and tarpaulin cover.

Pipe transport

MS, ERW, seamless, DI and HDPE pipes up to 12 m, chocked and end-protected.

03

Scrap transport

HMS, shredded and industrial scrap from source to mills, with weight reconciliation.

Bulk transport

Container transport
